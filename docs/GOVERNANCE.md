# Governance

FaveLists is a single-maintainer project.

- **Decisions** are made by the maintainer (see
  [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md)) and recorded in
  [DECISIONS.md](../DECISIONS.md); the project's standing rules are
  in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md).
- **Contributions** are welcomed under
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md); acceptance is the
  maintainer's call, with the library-first architecture rule and
  the full test gate as hard requirements.
- **Releases** are never automatic: a tag and GitHub release happen
  only on the maintainer's explicit confirmation, and fixes are
  batched into grouped releases rather than shipped one at a time.
- **Scope** is fixed by the project's purpose: single-type lists of
  favorites across the 11 fixed categories, local-first, with
  online auto-fill. No accounts, no sync, no user-created
  categories.
- **Licence**: MIT (see [LICENSE](../LICENSE)). The software is
  free; optional donations never unlock anything, and there are no
  ads or in-app purchases.
